RustBrowser

A small browser engine written from scratch in Rust.

Type a URL or search terms in the bar above and press Enter. Pages are parsed, styled, laid out and painted by this program, and their scripts run in the Boa JavaScript engine.

Works best on simple pages. Sites that depend on flexbox, grid, positioning or images will render as a plain stack of blocks.

What the engine handles

  1. HTML tokenizing and tree building, with implied end tags for p, li, table cells and others
  2. CSS: type, class, ID, attribute, descendant and child selectors; specificity; !important; @media width queries
  3. Inheritance and the cascade across the built-in stylesheet, <style>, linked sheets and style=""
  4. Block layout with margins, padding, borders, max-width, auto-margin centering and margin collapsing between siblings
    • Inline layout with word wrapping, text-align, line-height and white-space: pre
    • Bold, italic and monospace fonts; underline and strike-through
  5. Ordered and unordered lists, links, fragment anchors and history
  6. JavaScript with a live DOM: events, timers, fetch, forms, localStorage and history.pushState
